Features for accurate tracking and process optimization

Track progress visually

Plan tasks, milestones, and workflows with Gantt charts and Boards; then use burndown charts to stay on target.

Visualize your project's progress at a glance with intuitive charts that show you exactly where you stand. Compare actual progress against estimates and ideal trajectories to identify bottlenecks early.

Track important tasks with the Watch feature

Watch tasks to keep closer tabs on them — whether it's assigned to you or not, you'll get updates on changes.

Never miss an update on critical tasks, even if they're not directly assigned to you. The Watch feature keeps you informed about any changes or progress updates.

Stay organized with Tasks

Break down large projects and tasks into subtasks to move work forward in logical stages.

Complex projects become manageable when broken down into smaller, actionable subtasks.

Quickly create tasks with issue templates

Create custom task templates, so you can generate common tasks quickly.

Save time and ensure consistency by creating templates for frequently used task types. Standardize the format and required information for bugs, features, design requests, and more.
